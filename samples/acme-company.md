# Acme Corporation Overview

Acme Corporation was founded in 2018 by Alice Chen and Bob Kumar in San Francisco.

Alice Chen is the CEO of Acme Corporation. Bob Kumar serves as the Chief Technology Officer.

Acme Corporation acquired Beta Labs in 2021. Beta Labs is known for the Orion Analytics Platform.

The Orion Analytics Platform depends on the GraphRAG Engine developed internally by Acme's AI team.

Carol Diaz joined Acme Corporation in 2022 as Head of AI and leads development of the GraphRAG Engine.
